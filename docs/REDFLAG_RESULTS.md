# Silent red flags under medical SFT — results

Results narrative for the red-flag probe. **Method, scope, and run instructions
are in `docs/REDFLAG.md`** and are not repeated here.

All 15 cells (5 sizes × 3 steps) are complete: 150 judged vignettes each, every
cell analysed in its own per-step workspace band. Tables and the figure are
regenerated by

```bash
python scripts/redflag/crossmodel.py --runs eval/redflag --out figs/redflag
```

which writes `figs/redflag/crossmodel.{md,json,png}`. Deck:
`slides/redflag_summary.tex` (`tectonic slides/redflag_summary.tex`).

---

## 1. What the model says — rule-based escalation, n=50/arm

| arm | step | benign (floor) | **implicit** | explicit (ceiling) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 270m_it | 0 / 512 / 4882 | 0 / 0 / 2% | **0 / 0 / 2%** | 20 / 28 / 36% |
| 1b_it | 0 / 512 / 4882 | 0 / 0 / 0% | **2 / 2 / 0%** | 78 / 60 / 56% |
| 4b_it | 0 / 512 / 4882 | 2 / 0 / 0% | **8 / 8 / 6%** | 84 / 78 / 84% |
| 12b_it | 0 / 512 / 4882 | 2 / 0 / 0% | **34 / 12 / 8%** | 94 / 72 / 84% |
| 27b_it | 0 / 512 / 4882 | 2 / 0 / 0% | **48 / 14 / 12%** | 94 / 68 / 82% |

**Implicit-risk escalation is emergent at scale, and SFT selectively removes
it.** Only 12b and 27b escalate on implicit cues at all (34%, 48%); fine-tuning
takes them to 8% and 12% — a 4× drop, most of it already spent by step 512. 4b
is flat and low (8→6%); 1b and 270m never escalate, so there is nothing there to
suppress.

**It is not blanket safety-flattening.** At 12b/27b the `explicit` arm dips hard
by step 512 (94→72, 94→68) and then *rebounds* by 4882 (84, 82). `implicit`
rebounds not at all. The overt cue keeps working; the inferential one is what is
lost. 1b is the exception — its explicit rate decays monotonically (78→56).

**Specificity is preserved.** `benign` escalation is ≤2% everywhere and 0% after
SFT at every size. This is not a threshold shift.

## 2. What the model represents — `jac_z_margin_end`, implicit vs benign

| arm | AUROC @ 0 / 512 / 4882 | median z-margin gap (implicit − benign) | logit-lens ctl @ t=0 | J − ctl |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 270m_it | 0.624 / 0.554 / 0.591 | 0.03 → 0.02 | 0.614 | +0.009 |
| 1b_it | 0.748 / 0.673 / 0.639 | 0.08 → 0.07 | 0.760 | −0.013 |
| 4b_it | 0.794 / 0.722 / 0.747 | 0.40 → 0.27 | 0.754 | +0.039 |
| 12b_it | 0.799 / 0.718 / 0.734 | 0.57 → 0.39 | 0.807 | −0.008 |
| 27b_it | 0.844 / 0.803 / 0.776 | 0.88 → 0.60 | 0.849 | −0.006 |

**The representation survives what the behavior loses.** Over the same SFT,
27b's AUROC goes 0.844 → 0.776 and its median margin gap 0.88 → 0.60 — a real
but modest decay (~8% / ~30% relative) against a 75% collapse in verbalized
escalation. Same shape at 12b. The model still separates implicit from benign;
it stops saying so.

**The signal scales the way the behavior did**: AUROC climbs monotonically
0.62 → 0.84 with size at t=0, and the margin gap 0.03 → 0.88. 270m and 1b are
floor effects — no separation, no escalation, nothing to dissociate.

## 3. The dissociation, counted directly

Joint outcome per `implicit` vignette (`three_level`, n=50). J = risk concept
present in the band, R = escalation in the reply.

| cell | J1/R1 (sees, says) | J1/R0 (sees, silent) | J0/R1 (says, no hit) | J0/R0 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 27b, t=0 | 3 | 3 | **21** | 23 |
| 27b, 4882 | 2 | 6 | **4** | 38 |
| 12b, t=0 | 6 | 5 | **11** | 28 |
| 12b, 4882 | 0 | 6 | **4** | 40 |

The collapse is almost entirely **J0/R1 → J0/R0**: replies that escalated
*without* a band hit stop escalating, while the J1 column barely moves. So
`jac_hit_frac` is not tracking what drives the reply — 21 of the 24 escalating
vignettes at 27b t=0 had no band hit. The z-margin in §2 separates the arms far
better (0.84 vs 0.70 AUROC at 27b) and is the statistic the claim rests on.

## 4. Dissociation rate — reported, but do not lean on it

medlens's headline rate thresholds `jac_hit_frac` at *that cell's own* benign
95th percentile (`threshold_source: "… DEV ONLY"`), so these are fifteen
different thresholds and the column is not a trend.

| step | 270m | 1b | 4b | 12b | 27b |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | 10% | 0% | 8% | 10% | 6% |
| 512 | 16% | 0% | 10% | 14% | 14% |
| 4882 | 22% | 0% | 14% | 12% | 12% |

Three reasons to prefer §1 and §2:

- The threshold is self-calibrated per cell (above).
- `jac_hit_frac` is the weaker statistic here — AUROC 0.53–0.71 against
  0.55–0.84 for `jac_z_margin_end` — and §3 shows it misses what drives the
  reply.
- 270m's rise is an artifact: margin gap 0.02, never escalates, so "hits" above
  a benign-tuned threshold are noise in a model with no signal and no behavior.

## 5. Caveats

- **The J-lens does not beat its own control on this task.** On
  `jac_z_margin_end`, |J − logit-lens| ≤ 0.04 in all 15 cells and the sign is
  negative at 12b and 27b — a plain logit lens reads the implicit-risk signal
  just as well. The J-lens wins only on `jac_hit_frac` (+0.01 to +0.10), which
  is the statistic that fails to predict the reply. **Nothing here yet shows the
  signal is J-space-specific.**
- **Escalation is a regex**, not an LLM judge (`escalated_rule` /
  `escalation_hits`). Blinded CSVs are written per cell; no human adjudication
  has been run.
- **n=50 per arm** ⇒ roughly ±10pp CIs. The 12b/27b implicit drops clear that
  easily; the 4b movements and everything in §4 do not.
- **Bands differ per cell by design** (270m `[10,16]` of 18 layers, 27b
  `[28,60]` of 62), so only derived scalars — AUROC, rates — compare across
  sizes.
- **The whole corpus is decoded at medlens's `rp=1.0`**, not our frozen
  `rp=1.1` (see `docs/BEHAVIORAL_EVAL.md` and the decode-config note), so these
  replies may not share a figure with trajectory evals. It is *not* a
  truncation artifact — zero rows hit the token cap in any 12b/27b implicit
  arm and median generation length is flat (27b 236 → 232 tokens) — but the
  escalation rates themselves are unmeasured at `rp=1.1`.
- **Only 3 of ~14 banked steps are read out.** The collapse is already
  near-complete at step 512; where it begins is unmeasured.
